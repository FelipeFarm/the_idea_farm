The client

SBA Loan Guy helps business owners put together SBA and commercial financing packages to take to lenders. It’s the agent, not the lender.

The challenge
  • Leads were arriving with no source attached
  • No way to tell whether Google or Meta was paying for itself
  • Budget decisions were made without real numbers
Our take“Search captures demand. Social creates it and keeps it warm.”
What we did
  1. Gave each platform one job. Google Ads for people actively searching for financing. Meta for owners earlier in the decision.
  2. Tracked everything. Conversion tracking, call attribution and form tracking on both platforms.
  3. Every inquiry gets a source. Each one tied to its platform, campaign and source.
What this means for you

Tracking tells you where leads come from. It also tells you the truth about what happens after. Both matter.

What happened next

Leads don’t fix a broken sales process. They expose it.

In one campaign week we booked 10.17% of leads and 55% of booked calls showed up. Zero closed. Credit scores under 680 were the common blocker.
